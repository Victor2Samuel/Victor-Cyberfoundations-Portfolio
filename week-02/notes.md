## In My Own Words

**What is the cybersecurity landscape, and why does it matter?**

```
The cybersecurity landscape is the larger overview of the digital environment and the various elements that makes up that landscape. The attackers are the bad actors trying to break in steal and disrupt the landscape, defenders are the good guys preventing the attackers from achieving their goals by securing the landscape, infrastructure is the asset that needs to be protected and the everyday users are everyone using the infrastructure to perform one task or another.
```

**How do hardware, OS, and software layers interact?**

```
Software sends a request for a specific task. Operating system receives the request, translate and sends it to the hardware and The Hardware is the physical device, it does the heavy lifting, it receives instructions / tasks to be performed from the OS.
```
